## 1. Product Vision
AURA is designed to be the world's most luxurious, seamless, and high-performance digital advertising marketplace. It unites brands, advertising agencies, billboard owners, digital publishers, influencers, and creators into a single, cohesive, transaction-safe ecosystem.

AURA visualizes advertising not as fragmented, offline negotiations, but as an elegant, programmatic asset class. By replacing manual paperwork, opaque pricing, and disconnected communication channels with an ultra-fluid, real-time platform, AURA empowers brands to plan, negotiate, purchase, and analyze high-impact multi-channel campaigns (Digital, Out-Of-Home, Influencer, Print, Audio) within a unified visual environment.

The platform's interface is designed as an architectural masterpiece—emulating the technical sophistication of the Vercel and Stripe Dashboards, the creative gig fluidity of Fiverr, the bespoke enterprise contracting of Upwork, the professional authority of LinkedIn, and the seamless calendar booking of Airbnb and Booking.com.

## 2. Mission
To professionalize, digitize, and scale the advertising industry in Iran and emerging markets, and then bridge them to the global economy. AURA provides brands with direct, transparent, and beautiful software tools to procure premier advertising spaces and talent without intermediaries, while giving media owners and publishers an elite dashboard to maximize yield, manage contracts, and streamline their entire operation.

## 3. Core Goals
*   **Programmatic Standardization of Offline Assets (OOH):** Transform physical billboards, transit advertising, and mall displays into digital, bookable inventory with predictable calendars, pricing tiers, and verifiable impressions.
*   **Unified Multi-Channel Campaign Orchestration:** Enable a brand to launch a coordinated campaign across 50 digital publishers, 10 key physical billboards, and 12 premium influencers from a single, beautifully integrated multi-step checkout.
*   **Trust and Escrow Security:** Implement a highly secure, transaction-safe escrow system (Better Auth, multi-layered smart-contracts, and automated verification) that protects publishers and advertisers, ensuring zero payment default.
*   **Unparalleled Analytical Clarity:** Provide real-time reporting interfaces using FastAPI WebSockets and interactive, high-fidelity Three.js graphics to track performance, impressions, CTRs, and ROI.
*   **Frictionless Global Integration:** Architect the entire system from Day 1 to support multi-currency (Toman and USD), multilingual (Persian and English), and cross-border payment structures to facilitate effortless international media procurement.

## 6. Business Model
AURA acts as a **Managed transactional marketplace** and **SaaS-Enabled Marketplace (SEM)**.

Unlike traditional open directories, AURA sits directly in the transactional flow. It provides premium programmatic tools (SaaS) for media owners to manage their inventory and schedule, while charging a service/commission fee on every successful match made on the marketplace. By offering "Vercel-level" deployment for ad placements and "Stripe-level" payment flow, AURA becomes the operating system for both sides of the market.

---

## 7. Revenue Model
To build a sustainable multi-billion-dollar enterprise, AURA leverages multiple synergistic monetization vectors:

1.  **Marketplace Transaction Fee (Take Rate):**
    *   **Advertiser Side:** 3% platform facilitation fee on all transactions.
    *   **Publisher/Creator Side:** 8% to 15% marketplace commission, scaling down as the transaction value increases (e.g., enterprise OOH packages over 10 Billion Toman fall to a flat 5%).
2.  **SaaS Subscriptions (AURA Pro & AURA Enterprise):**
    *   **For Media Owners:** Elite inventory tracking software, automated dynamic pricing engines, advanced heatmaps, and Web Audio/3D preview generation.
    *   **For Advertisers:** Advanced multi-channel analytics, AI-powered copywriting/media assistant, multi-user workspace management, and white-label reporting exports.
3.  **Promoted Listings (AURA Launch/Product Hunt style):**
    *   Publishers and Influencers can pay to boost their listings or display premium "Verified Prime" badges on the marketplace search page.
4.  **Escrow & Financing (AURA Capital):**
    *   Factoring and trade financing options where OOH publishers can get immediate payouts on confirmed booking contracts, with AURA taking a financing fee of 2-4%.

## 15. Core Features Overview

### A. The Unified Multichannel Marketplace Directory
An interactive, high-fidelity catalog utilizing custom-developed map integrations and 3D mockups.
*   **Interactive 3D Billboard Previews:** Using React Three Fiber and Three.js, advertisers can upload their creative asset and instantly preview how it looks on a digital highway billboard or building facade under different lighting conditions (Day, Night, Rainy) before booking.
*   **Fiverr-Style Creator Gig Cards:** Creators list predefined "Ad Deliverables" (e.g., "1x Instagram Reels + Story Package") with locked pricing, deliverables, and turnaround times.
*   **Booking.com-Style Reservation Calendars:** Real-time availability matrices for billboard spots, native website banner positions, and newsletter slots, showing current bookings and high-demand pricing tiers.

### B. High-Fidelity Escrow & Contract Suite
An elite portal that handles negotiations, custom agreements, and milestone transactions.
*   **Milestone-Based Escrow Vaults:** Funding is committed upfront by the advertiser and locked in an automated platform vault. Capital is released programmatically (e.g., 20% on contract sign, 40% on media proof upload, 40% on campaign completion).
*   **Verification Engine:** Publishers upload visual proof (time-stamped high-res photo of physical billboard or digital analytics report file). The system verifies metadata and submits it for advertiser sign-off.
*   **Upwork-Style Dispute Resolution:** A premium arbitrated channel with dedicated AURA legal and marketing account managers for high-ticket campaigns.

The system is split into five elite, fully integrated modules.

### Module 1: The Brand Workspace (The Advertiser Hub)
Designed to resemble the Vercel Dashboard combined with a premium portfolio analytics interface.
*   **Campaign Builder:** A multi-step flow where advertisers set target budgets, choose dates, and select inventory types.

### Module 3: Aura Live (The Real-Time Engine)
*   **Collaborative Live Editor:** Enables brands and agency partners to annotate designs, leave pinpoint comments on media mockups, and negotiate pricing changes live over persistent WebSocket connections.
*   **Notification Matrix:** Clean toast messages and push notifications confirming contract progression, asset approvals, and payment disbursements.

### Module 4: Design System (AURA.ui)
Built on Tailwind CSS v4, Framer Motion, and Radix UI.
*   **Glassmorphic Shell:** Deep backdrops (`bg-neutral-950/85`), heavy blur (`backdrop-blur-xl`), and delicate high-contrast borders (`border-white/10`).
*   **The Royal Horizon Color Palette:**
    *   *Base:* Deep Black (`#09090b`), Graphite (`#18181b`), Dark Navy (`#020617`).

*   **Micro-Interactions:** Custom spring-physics hover animations, beautiful border-glow effects, and fluid dashboard page transitions using Framer Motion.

### Module 5: Infrastructure & Security (The Core Engine)
*   **Better Auth Guard:** Seamless OAuth, professional magic link logins, and fine-grained Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) (Advertiser, Publisher, Creator, Admin).
*   **Pydantic/Zod Strict Validation Pipeline:** Ensures every incoming API request on FastAPI matches strict data parameters, preventing database corruption or unauthorized price mutations.
*   **Neon Serverless DB Architecture:** Highly scalable serverless PostgreSQL database with automated branching for testing and instant vertical scaling.

## 17. Risks and Mitigation

### Risk 1: Platform Bypass (Disintermediation)
*   **Description:** Advertisers and creators match on the platform but take payment offline to avoid service fees.
*   **Mitigation:**
    1.  *Managed Escrow Value:* The transaction is only legally backed and contractually guaranteed if handled through the platform escrow.
    2.  *Built-In Collaboration & Productivity Tools:* The workspace, feedback canvas, and live tracking are so useful that conducting campaigns offline introduces massive friction.
    3.  *Tiered Commissions:* Lower the platform fee dramatically for high-ticket and long-term returning clients.

### Risk 2: Compliance & Local Regulations
*   **Description:** OOH advertising and digital campaigns are subject to strict regulatory oversight and ministry guidelines in Iran.
*   **Mitigation:** Built-in automated workflow gates where publisher assets cannot be booked unless the client provides verified regulatory approvals (e.g., وزارت فرهنگ و ارشاد اسلامی permissions).

### Risk 3: Internet Interruptions & Offline Failures
*   **Description:** Connection drops or localized API latency.
*   **Mitigation:** Robust caching strategies via local IndexedDB (managed through Zustand persisting state), progressive enhancement rendering, and offline fallback screens.

```
                       │       CLOUDFLARE CDN /        │

                       └───────────────┬───────────────┘

                                       ▼

                       │    NEON PG (SERVERLESS) +     │
                       │     ROBUST PRISMA CACHE       │

```

*   **Static Pre-Rendering & ISR:** Directory lists, seller profiles, and programmatic inventory cards are statically generated using Next.js 16 Incremental Static Regeneration (ISR). This guarantees 100ms loading speeds for search engines and guest visitors, bypassing the database for static hits.
*   **Stateless FastAPI Microservices:** FastAPI handles calculation and validation without state. Fast API replicas can scale horizontally on Railway instantly to handle high traffic surges.
*   **Neon Serverless Database Pooling:** Dynamic connections are routed through Neon's serverless connection pool, dynamically scaling PG instances in milliseconds and shutting down to zero during quiet hours to optimize cost.
*   **Optimized Asset Delivery:** Media assets, high-res billboard photography, and video proofs are optimized on the fly and distributed globally through Cloudflare R2, keeping the core web server fast and unencumbered.
